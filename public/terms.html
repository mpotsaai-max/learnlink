9. Monthly Packages and Subscriptions

9.1 Package Creation

Tutors may create Monthly Packages offering a fixed number of Sessions per month at a discounted rate compared to individual Session bookings. Package terms, including price, number of Sessions included, subjects covered, and validity period, are set by the Tutor and displayed on their profile.

9.2 Subscription Terms

When a Student subscribes to a Monthly Package, they are purchasing a bundle of Sessions valid for one calendar month from the subscription start date. Sessions included in a package must be used within the subscription period. Unused Sessions do not roll over to the next month and are not refundable.

9.3 Commission on Packages

The 15% Commission applies to the total Monthly Package price. For example, if a Tutor offers a package for P800, LearnLink retains P120 and pays the Tutor P680.

9.4 Cancellation

Monthly Packages are non-refundable once purchased, except where the Tutor fails to deliver the agreed number of Sessions due to the Tutor's cancellation or no-show. In such cases, a pro-rata refund may be issued at LearnLink's discretion.
