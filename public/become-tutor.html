Commission structure

LearnLink takes a 15% commission on every booking. You receive 85% of your listed rate. For example, if you charge P200 per session, you will receive P170 and we keep P30. You are paid within 24 hours after each completed session. All sessions are held online via video call.

Each session is 1 hour. You set your own rate. You will receive 85% after commission.
